PROJECT TITLE: SvaraPrana (स्वरप्राण) — Zero-Hardware Ambient Acoustic Care Bridge & Non-Invasive Healthcare Coordination

1. THE PROBLEM:
Over 60 million elderly individuals in India live independently or away from immediate family support. Current eldercare solutions face three critical adoption barriers:
- The Privacy vs. Surveillance Paradox: Continuous monitoring solutions (in-home cameras, radar, ambient sensors) feel intrusive and police-like, leading elderly individuals to actively resist or disconnect them due to dignity and privacy concerns.
- Technology & Language Friction: Most digital health apps are designed with small fonts, multi-nested menus, and English-dominant text interfaces that alienate seniors who have limited digital literacy or regional language preferences.
- The "False Compliance" Blindspot: Remote families struggle to verify medication adherence and routine wellness because elders frequently report false compliance ("Yes, I took it") to prevent their working children from worrying.

2. PROPOSED SOLUTION:
SvaraPrana is a zero-hardware, privacy-first healthcare coordination platform that transforms standard cellular phone calls into an ambient health diagnostic and family care bridge:

A. Ambient Family Call Bridge (Passive Vitality Monitoring):
During ordinary calls between family members and elderly parents, SvaraPrana operates silently in the background over standard cellular (PSTN) or VoIP channels. An on-server audio Digital Signal Processing (DSP) engine extracts non-semantic acoustic telemetry in transient memory—analyzing vocal jitter, shimmer, phoneme latency, and speech pacing. It detects early motor fatigue, speech slurring, and dehydration. 
- Absolute Privacy: Spoken words are never transcribed, recorded, or stored; raw audio buffers are purged within milliseconds of feature extraction.

B. Morning Routine & Medication Companion (Active Coordination):
When families are unavailable, an automated 45-second check-in call connects with the elder in their native regional language (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, etc.). It delivers a culturally resonant greeting, verifies medication adherence via voice or single-digit IVR touch, and conducts non-invasive respiratory flow checks.
- Dignity-First Handling: If the elder is busy, grumbles, or declines to answer, the system never pesters or loops robotically; it politely disconnects and seamlessly falls back to passive monitoring during evening family calls.
- Cognitive Hesitation Tracking: Detects voice latency and uncertainty markers to flag potential skipped doses ("courtesy reporting") without confronting the parent.

3. WHO IT IS FOR (TARGET PERSONA):
- Primary Persona: Elderly parents living independently across Tier-1 to Tier-3 towns, using either basic 2G feature/keypad phones or simple smartphones, who prefer regional language communication.
- Secondary Persona: Adult children working away from home who require structured peace of mind, shared care coordination, and objective wellness indicators without infringing upon their parents autonomy.

4. WHY THIS SOLUTION SHOULD EXIST:
- ₹0 Capital Expenditure & Zero New Hardware: Operates over existing cellular telecom infrastructure on any basic mobile phone; requires no smartwatches, smartbands, or cameras.
- Zero Behavioral Change: Seniors do not need to learn a new app, navigate touchscreens, or adopt unfamiliar gadgets.
- Non-Invasive Family Visibility: Caregivers access a streamlined "Vitality Index" (stability, respiratory cadence, medication status) without intrusive live surveillance feeds.
- Tiered Escalation Protocols: Unanswered check-in cycles or acute voice distress signatures trigger automated IVR follow-ups to primary family contacts and local emergency responders.
